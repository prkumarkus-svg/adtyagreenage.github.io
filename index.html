What we do

Grassing is our main work. We handle the ground preparation around it so the grass grows evenly and lasts.

Stadium turf

Turf laying and seeding for football, cricket and multi-sport stadiums.

Ground levelling

Grading and soil preparation so the playing surface is flat and drains well.

Drainage and irrigation

Drain lines and watering systems planned for the size of your ground.

Re-turfing and repair

Worn centres, goal mouths and patches renewed between seasons.

Parks and open grounds

Grassing for parks, school grounds, golf areas and campus lawns.

Ongoing ground care

Mowing, feeding, aeration and overseeding on a schedule that suits you.

How a project runs

Every ground is different, so we start by looking at yours.

  1. Site visit. We check soil, slope, drainage, sunlight and water supply.
  2. Quote and plan. You get a clear price, grass type and timeline.
  3. Preparation. We level, improve the soil and set up drainage.
  4. Grassing. Turf is laid or seeded with the right variety for your climate and sport.
  5. Handover and care. We tell you how to water and mow, and we can maintain it for you.
